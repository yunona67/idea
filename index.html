MY STEAM PROJECT · 7TH GRADE

Ready-Glow
Folder

A simple invention to make getting ready for school easier—and help students remember what to bring.

Student: Your First and Last Name Class: 7th Grade

🤔The Problem

Many students forget a notebook, homework sheet, or pencil when they are rushing to school. It can be stressful to discover something is missing when class has already started.

📓+ ✏️+ 🎒

💡My Idea

My idea is a folder with a reusable checklist and a bright color tab for each school day. Before leaving home, students check their list and slide the tab to green when they are ready.

⚙️How It Works

The Ready-Glow Folder keeps a checklist right where students can see it. It could be made from a regular folder, a paper checklist, and a small sliding piece of colored card.

  • 1Write down the things you need for school.
  • 2Put each item in your folder or backpack.
  • 3Slide the tab to green when everything is packed!

🌱Who Will It Help?

It can help students who get distracted, feel rushed, or have lots to remember. Families may find mornings calmer, and students can feel more prepared and confident at school.

Made with imagination, a little paper, and a big idea ✨
